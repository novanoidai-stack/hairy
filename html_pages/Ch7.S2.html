7.2.3 Rate as a Population Activity (Average over Several Neurons)

The number of neurons in the brain is huge. Often many neurons have similar properties and respond to the same stimuli. For example, neurons in the primary visual cortex of cats and monkeys are arranged in columns of cells with similar properties (230). Let us idealize the situation and consider a population of neurons with identical properties. In particular, all neurons in the population should have the same pattern of input and output connections. The spikes of the neurons in a population mm are sent off to another population nn. In our idealized picture, each neuron in population nn receives input from all neurons in population mm.
